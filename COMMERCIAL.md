# Commercial use of QuickAI

QuickAI is free for personal and other noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Using it in or for a business needs a commercial license.

## Do I need one?

| You are | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Using QuickAI for yourself: learning, writing, hobby projects, private use | Nothing, it's free |
| A student, teacher or researcher doing study or public research | Nothing, it's free |
| A charity, school, university, public research body, public health or safety organization, environmental organization or government institution | Nothing, it's free |
| A company, freelancer or sole trader using QuickAI for your work, internally or for clients | A commercial license |
| Shipping QuickAI, or a product built on it, to customers | A commercial license |
| Offering QuickAI as part of a paid service | A commercial license |

The license text decides; this table is a plain-language summary of it. If you are not sure which row you are in,
ask. A short email usually settles it.

## What a commercial license covers

- Using QuickAI's own code in your organization, on your desktops and on your servers (`quickai-run`).
- Terms, scope and support agreed for your organization: how many people or machines, deployment help, and
  how updates reach you.

## How to get one

Write to **hello@everybodyai.dev**, or use the form at
[everybodyai.dev/organizations](https://everybodyai.dev/organizations). Tell us roughly who would use it and how.

## What it does not cover

A commercial license covers QuickAI's own code. Everything else keeps its own license, as listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md):

- **Bundled:** llama.cpp's `llama-server` (MIT) and the Bonsai 8B and 1.7B weights (Apache-2.0).
- **Downloaded only when you ask:** Ollama (MIT), ComfyUI (GPL-3.0) and Open WebUI (its own license).
- **Models you download:** each comes with its own license, and some do not allow commercial use or exclude
  some regions. Check a model's license before using it at work. The Recommended list shows each model's license.
